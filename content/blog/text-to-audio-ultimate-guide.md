## 🧠 What Is Text-to-Audio?

Text-to-audio, also called **text-to-speech (TTS)**, is an AI-powered technology that reads written content aloud. Today’s AI voice generators produce voices that sound **incredibly natural**, complete with emotions, accents, and tonal nuances — almost indistinguishable from real humans.  

---

## 🚀 Why Use Text-to-Audio?

Adopting TTS technology can **revolutionize your content strategy**. Here’s why:  

### ✅ Accessibility
Make your content available for people with visual impairments or learning differences.  

### ✅ Content Repurposing
Turn blogs, newsletters, or scripts into **podcasts, videos, or voiceovers**.  

### ✅ Engagement Boost
Audio content keeps users engaged, especially on mobile or when multitasking.  

### ✅ Time & Cost Efficiency
No need to hire voice actors — AI generates audio instantly.
